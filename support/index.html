Postcard Garden support

A little garden, a little joy.

Each letter in Postcard Garden is a small puzzle: a visitor asks for a garden, and you place the path, the flowers and the garden pieces so that every wish is met. Solve it and the garden becomes a postcard.

Common questions

How do I turn a piece?
Tap the piece again in the tray, or press the turn button. You can also turn it while you are dragging it.
I'm stuck on a letter.
Press the hint button. It's free and unlimited, and shows where one piece belongs. There are no timers and no lives, so take your time.
How do I move a piece I already placed?
Tap or drag it to lift it back up, or use Undo.
Is my progress saved?
Yes. As a guest it is saved on your device. Signing in with Google or Apple is optional.
Can I make the game calmer or easier to read?
Open Settings to turn music and sounds on or off, reduce motion, or use larger text.
Privacy
See the Privacy Policy linked from the App Store page. The game has no ads and no tracking.
