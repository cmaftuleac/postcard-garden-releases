Privacy Policy

Postcard Garden · last updated 20 November 2026

Postcard Garden is a calm garden puzzle game. It shows no ads, contains no in-app purchases, and does not track you across other apps or websites. This policy explains the little information the game uses.

Playing as a guest

If you play as a guest, nothing leaves your device. Your progress, settings and any unfinished garden are stored only on your phone. Deleting the app deletes them.

Signing in with Google or Apple (optional)

You can choose to sign in with Google or Apple. Sign-in is handled by Unity Player Accounts, a service run by Unity Technologies. When you sign in, Unity receives an account identifier from Google or Apple and may receive your email address, as Google or Apple allows you to choose. The game uses this only to recognise your account. Unity's own handling of this data is described in the Unity Player Privacy Policy. You can sign out at any time from the game's settings.

Postcards

When you save a postcard, the picture is saved on your device. The game does not upload it anywhere. You decide whether to share it.

Children

Postcard Garden is suitable for all ages. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children. A child can play the whole game as a guest without sharing any information.

What we do not do

  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information.
  • No tracking, no location, no contacts, no camera or microphone access.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions are welcome on the Postcard Garden support page.
